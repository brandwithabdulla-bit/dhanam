OUR STORY

WE DON'T BELIEVE
IN SHORTCUTS.

Dhanam Food Products was created from a simple but uncompromising belief: the food we use every day should be pure, safe and honestly made.

Our journey begins with carefully selected raw materials. Whether it is chilli, coriander, flour or another essential ingredient, we believe the quality of the final product begins with the quality of what goes into it.

"From Raw Material to Final Pack — Quality Under Our Care."

We plan to work with grade-one, premium-quality raw produce and carefully manage the important stages of processing ourselves. The raw materials are cleaned and washed thoroughly, hygienically dried using controlled drying methods, and carefully ground through our milling process.

Dhanam Food Products is being built around purity, transparency, quality and responsibility.

PERFECT PUTTU.
IN JUST A FEW MINUTES.

Traditional Kerala breakfast made simple.

FROM OUR MILLS
TO YOUR MORNING TABLE.

We believe traditional food does not need to disappear in a busy modern life. With carefully processed ingredients and convenient formats, Dhanam helps bring familiar Kerala flavours back into everyday kitchens.

01

MIX

Take one glass of Dhanam Puttu Podi. Add salt according to taste and gradually mix with approximately one glass of water until properly moistened.

02

LAYER

Add grated coconut and the moistened puttu podi in alternating layers into the puttu kutti.

03

STEAM

Steam for approximately 5–7 minutes.

04

SERVE

Serve hot with banana, jaggery or kadala curry for a classic Kerala breakfast.
